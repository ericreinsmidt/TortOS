# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** -
- **Superseded by:** -

## Context

What is true that forces a decision. Facts and constraints, not opinions. If a
fact was measured, say so and give the number. If it was assumed, say that too -
an assumption recorded as an assumption can be re-checked; one recorded as a
fact cannot.

## Options considered

### Option A - name
What it is, why it is plausible, what it costs.

### Option B - name
Same. Include the option you rejected, honestly argued. A one-sided ADR is a
press release, and the rejected option is the one future-you will want to
reconsider.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder - this half matters more and is the
half people skip. What this forecloses.

## Revisit if

The specific, checkable condition that reopens this. Not "if it becomes a
problem" - that is a hope, not a trigger.
